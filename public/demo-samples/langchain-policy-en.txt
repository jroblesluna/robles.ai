NORTHWIND LOGISTICS — TRAVEL AND REMOTE WORK POLICY (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)
Version 3.2 · Effective January 1, 2026 · Owner: People Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy explains how Northwind Logistics employees may work remotely and how business travel is requested, approved and reimbursed.

2. REMOTE WORK
2.1 Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
2.2 Tuesdays are "anchor days": every team works from the office on Tuesdays unless their manager approves an exception in writing.
2.3 Employees working remotely must be reachable on the company chat between 9:00 and 16:00 local time.
2.4 Northwind provides a one-time home office allowance of USD 400 for a chair, desk or monitor. Receipts must be submitted within 60 days of purchase.
2.5 Remote work from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year and requires approval from People Operations.

3. BUSINESS TRAVEL — APPROVALS
3.1 Domestic trips must be approved by the employee's direct manager at least 7 days before departure.
3.2 International trips must be approved by the direct manager AND the department director at least 21 days before departure.
3.3 Trips booked with less notice require a written justification from the manager.

4. BUSINESS TRAVEL — EXPENSES
4.1 Flights: economy class for trips under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more.
4.2 Hotels: up to USD 180 per night in standard cities and up to USD 260 per night in New York, London, Tokyo and San Francisco.
4.3 Meals: a daily allowance of USD 65 for domestic trips and USD 85 for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
4.4 Ground transport: taxis and ride-sharing are reimbursed for business purposes; car rentals require prior approval.
4.5 All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days after the trip ends, with itemized receipts for any expense above USD 25.

5. SAFETY
5.1 Travelers must register every trip in the Northwind travel portal so the company can contact them in an emergency.
5.2 Travel to destinations with a government "do not travel" advisory is not permitted.
